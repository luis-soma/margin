    One gesture keeps the work.
    One line keeps the reason.

    1. CaptureDouble-press ⌘ on a Figma frame, a website, some code or any part of the screen.
    2. Add the whyA small card asks for one line, typed or spoken. Esc keeps just the capture.
    3. Read it backZoom the timeline from close-up to big picture, sort captures into themes and hand the story to a coding agent.
    Is it free?Yes. Margin is free and open source.
    Where do captures live?On your Mac, in Margin’s own folder.
    Does it use AI?Only with your own API key, and only once you turn it on.
    Why the security prompt?Margin isn’t notarized yet. Allow it once in Privacy & Security.
