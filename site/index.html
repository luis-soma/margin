Document your design process while you work.

Margin is a Mac app for designers. Double-press ⌘ on whatever you’re working on, add one line about why, and it lands in an archive you can read from a single step to the whole project.

Download for Mac macOS 13 or later · Apple silicon and Intel · free

Margin isn’t notarized yet. The first time you open it, allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Your agent takes care of that for you.

How it works

  1. Capture. One gesture for everything: double-press ⌘. A Figma frame, a website, a selection, a screen area or a short recording, saved with where it came from.
  2. Add the why. A small card asks for one line, typed or spoken. Esc keeps just the capture.
  3. Keep documenting. Reminder or Automatic: Margin asks after a quiet stretch, or keeps a capture whenever your design moves on.
  4. Read it back. A timeline you zoom from close-up to big picture, and Themes, where Margin suggests groups and you decide what they mean.
  5. Hand it off. The project as a folder your coding agent can read: the story by day, your decisions, the look and every capture.
