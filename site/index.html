How Margin works

One gesture to keep the work, one line to keep the reason.

  1. 01
    CaptureDouble-press ⌘ on a Figma frame, a website, a selection or any part of the screen.
  2. 02
    Add the whyA small card asks for one line, typed or spoken. Esc keeps just the capture.
  3. 03
    Keep documentingReminder asks after a quiet stretch. Automatic keeps a capture when your design moves on.
  4. 04
    Read it backZoom the timeline from close-up to big picture. Themes suggests groups and you decide what they mean.
  5. 05
    Hand it offYour project as a folder a coding agent can read: the story by day, your decisions and every capture.
Is it free?Yes. Margin is free and open source.
Where do captures live?On your Mac, in Margin’s own folder, not in iCloud.
Does it use AI?Only with your own API key, and only once you turn it on.
Why the security prompt?Margin isn’t notarized yet. Allow it once in Privacy & Security.
