Margin for macOS 13 and later, Apple silicon and Intel.

**Install with your agent:** tell it "Install Margin by following https://raw.githubusercontent.com/luis-soma/margin/main/INSTALL.md".

**Or by hand:** download Margin.dmg below, drag Margin to Applications and open it. Margin isn't notarized: the first time, allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security (Open Anyway).

No API keys are inside. AI notes, headlines and themes are optional and use your own Gemini key.
